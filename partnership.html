Partner Program Terms

Clear rules for commission and reward eligibility.

  • Rewards are milestone-based.
  • Only one milestone reward can be claimed.
  • A partner can choose to wait and accumulate earnings toward a higher milestone.
  • Once a milestone is claimed, earlier accumulated earnings cannot be used to claim another reward from the same earning period.
  • Commission is applicable only to qualifying paid client projects.
  • Referral must be genuine and traceable to the partner.
  • Commission and reward eligibility are verified manually.
  • Rewards are subject to product availability.
  • Products may be substituted with another item of similar value if necessary.
  • Smartphone reward applies to the ₹25,000 milestone and is subject to the stated budget.
  • Shipping is provided free for eligible reward deliveries within serviceable locations, subject to applicable conditions.
  • The reward program can be updated or discontinued for future referrals with updated terms.
  • Any tax, regulatory or legal obligations applicable to commissions or rewards are the responsibility of the relevant party where required.
  • Vision Web Tech reserves the right to reject fraudulent, duplicate, self-referral or misleading referrals.
  • Final commission calculations are verified manually before payment.
  • Payment and reward processing timelines should be communicated clearly to eligible partners.
